JSR Rice (Steam, Raw & Broken)

JSR rice is one of India's most popular medium-grain rice varieties, widely cultivated in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Available in three premium grades — Steam, Raw, and Broken — it is prized for its fluffy texture, consistent grain size, and natural aroma after cooking. Ideal for daily consumption, hotel industry bulk supply, and rice flour milling applications. We supply both polished and semi-polished varieties with FSSAI-compliant packaging.

JSR Steam Rice

Our premium JSR Steam Rice is processed using an advanced hydrothermal method that parboils the grain while it is still in its husk. This intricate process locks in essential vitamins and minerals, resulting in a grain that is not only highly nutritious but also exceptionally firm and non-sticky after cooking. When boiled, the grains expand significantly in length, making it a high-yield choice for commercial kitchens, hotels, and large-scale catering events. It is widely recognized for its consistent white texture, superior digestibility, and long shelf-life, making it one of our most demanded export-quality products for both domestic and international wholesalers.

JSR Raw Rice

JSR Raw Rice represents the purest form of the grain, freshly milled directly from the harvest without any heat or steam treatment. This traditional milling method preserves the natural, delicate aroma and the distinctively sweet flavor profile that JSR rice is famous for across South India. Because it is un-steamed, the grains retain a natural translucency and a soft, tender bite that is highly favored for authentic home-style meals and daily consumption. We source this directly from certified farmer clusters, ensuring that every bag contains polished, dust-free grains that meet the highest FSSAI food safety standards, providing families with a healthy and delicious staple.

JSR Broken Rice

Our JSR Broken Rice is a premium grade of broken grains that occur naturally during the high-speed milling and polishing process. Despite being smaller in size, these grains retain 100% of the same nutritional content, aroma, and flavor as the whole-grain JSR variety, offering a highly economical solution for both household and industrial use. It is the preferred choice for preparing traditional fermented batters like Idly and Dosa, as the smaller grain size facilitates faster soaking and smoother grinding. Additionally, it is widely utilized by food processing industries for making rice flour, snacks, and traditional desserts like Kheer, providing exceptional value without compromising on authentic quality.

Fresh Okra (Lady Finger)

Our export-quality Okra (Lady Finger) is grown using sustainable agricultural practices and harvested while young and tender to ensure that the pods are soft, vibrant green, and free from any fiber or seeds. We follow a strict selection process, choosing only pods of uniform length (typically 8cm to 12cm) that are free from any bruises or pest damage, meeting the high standards required by European and Gulf supermarket chains. The produce is pre-cooled and packed in breathable, eco-friendly cartons to maintain its freshness and prevent wilting during Air freight transit. By maintaining a "farm-to-airport" supply chain, we guarantee that our Okra retains its natural crunch and vitamin content, providing a premium fresh vegetable solution for health-conscious consumers worldwide.

Fresh Premium Indian Fruits

We export a premium selection of fresh Indian fruits that are harvested at peak ripeness and transported via specialized Air Cargo to preserve their natural flavor, color, and nutritional value. Our fruit portfolio includes the world-famous Alphonso and Banganapalli Mangoes, prized for their rich aroma and sweetness; export-grade Bananas sourced from the fertile farms of Kerala and Tamil Nadu; fresh Guava known for its high Vitamin C content; Sweet Lime (Mosambi) widely popular in Gulf and European markets for its refreshing taste; and Watermelon available in seedless export varieties. Each fruit is carefully graded, sorted, and packed in ventilated, food-safe cartons with full phytosanitary certification, ensuring a smooth customs clearance process and maximum freshness on arrival at any international destination.
