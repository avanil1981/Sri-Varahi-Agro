About Us

Global Reach, Local Integrity

Sri Varahi Agro Foods LLP is a premier B2B agro-export enterprise dedicated to delivering India's finest agricultural products to international markets.

By leveraging our strategic partnerships with trusted NGOs and local farmer networks, we ensure a direct, transparent supply chain that guarantees uncompromised quality and fair trade. Our focus on international export standards, coupled with advanced air and sea cargo logistics, makes us a reliable partner for global importers seeking consistency, scale, and excellence in agro-commodities.
